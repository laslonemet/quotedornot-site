Why doesn't ChatGPT mention my company?

AI assistants recommend businesses they can verify across consistent, third-party-shaped sources: your website structure, public listings, review sites, and content that states clearly what you do, where you operate, and for whom. If those signals are missing or inconsistent, the model has no reason to quote you, so it falls back to the names it sees most often, which are usually your competitors.

How do I know what AI says about my business?

You have to ask the models the questions your buyers actually ask, for example "who are the top managed IT providers in [your city]?" and record the verbatim answers. Doing this properly means probing every major model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) with a consistent question set and keeping dated evidence. Partly.
